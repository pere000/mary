This is sometimes informally called “memorylessness,” although Markovianity does not imply that the system has no memory; rather, any history relevant to future evolution must be represented in the present state. In the stricter probabilistic sense, memorylessness is a special property of probability distributions, possessed by the geometric distribution in the discrete case and the exponential distribution in the continuous case.

Evolutionary process

Science can describe an evolutionary lineage leading from earlier organisms toward increasingly complex hominins. But that continuity of biological descent does not by itself establish that consciousness is generated by the biological process. That latter claim is an additional theoretical interpretation. Indeed, contemporary consciousness literature recognizes that the evolutionary history of consciousness remains unresolved.

Thus, in this context, this article proposes that the evolutionary process progressively actualizes increasingly complex living organizations through lawful Markovian state transitions, until a biological organization capable of supporting human existence is reached. At that point, the organism constitutes the biological condition prepared for the conception of the human soul ex nihilo.
