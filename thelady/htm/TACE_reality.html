TACE: Reality (Draft)

The Causeless Manifestation designed the rules, and the rules themselves—and nothing else—carry out the task.

In TACE, the Causeless Manifestation is the ultimate foundation of being and, consequently, the ultimate foundation of evidence. Evidence cannot exist by itself; it presupposes an existent reality of which it is evidence.
Mathematics is understood as a system of lawful formal relations—rules through which determinations and transformations are intelligibly structured. Tegmark's mathematical ontology is therefore retained within Tegmark-Aquinas Conceptual Ecosystem (TACE) insofar as it provides the mathematical-formal dimension of the framework, while TACE situates that dimension within its broader ontological architecture.

1. Beyond the Reality of Forms — God

God / Necessary Being

God is not one Form among Forms and does not belong to the Realm of Forms as an entity within that realm. God is transcendent to the Realm of Forms and is the ultimate source and actualizer of being.


Prime Concepts:
Unmoved Mover: Primordial non-caused causa causalis
Prime Potency: Universal principle/domain of potentiality
Possibility within Prime Potency: Ens in potentia
Actualization: Transition from potency toward actuality
Actualized possibility: Ens in actu
Entity: In TACE: determinate ens in actu

Note: Not every ens in actu necessarily needs to be called an Entity in the TACE technical sense; Entity is the category of determinate ens in actu.

Form: The principle of actuality and determination through which a potential being is constituted as a determinate mode of being and thereby possesses its characteristic intelligible organization.
In short: Principle by which an entity is determined to be the kind of being that it is.
Substrate: The principle of potentiality that receives and supports Form, providing the ontological capacity through which Form can be instantiated as a determinate being.
In short: Principle of potentiality through which that Form can be received or instantiated.
Form × Substrate → constitutes the determinate actual being

Unmoved Mover: Primordial non-caused causa causalis.

Prime Potency: Universal principle/domain of potentiality.

Possibility: An ontologically possible determination within Prime Potency.

Ens in potentia: A being in potency; an ontologically possible being possessing unrealized potency toward actuality.

Actualization: The transition from potency toward actuality.

Actualized: Having undergone actualization and thereby possessing actuality.

Ens in actu: Being in actuality; an actualized being.

Determinate: Possessing a determinate mode of being specified through Form.

Entity: A determinate ens in actu.

Form: The principle of actuality and determination through which a potential being is constituted as a determinate mode of being and thereby possesses its characteristic intelligible organization.

Substrate: The principle of potentiality that receives and supports Form, providing the ontological capacity through which Form can be instantiated as a determinate being.

Form × Substrate: The constitutive relation through which Form and Substrate constitute a determinate actual being.

Hylomorphism: In TACE, every entity is necessarily hylomorphic.

Hylomorphic: Constituted according to the necessary relation of Form and Substrate.

Substance: A determinate actual being constituted through its hylomorphic principles.

Structural Possibility: A possibility belonging to the structural mode of potentiality.

Intentional Possibility: A possibility belonging to the intentional mode of potentiality.

Consciousness: A foundational TACE concept formally coextensive with Intentionality; Consciousness ↔ Intentionality.

Intentionality: A foundational TACE concept formally coextensive with Consciousness and entailing the intentional character; Intentionality ↔ Consciousness and Intentionality → Intentional.

Intentional: Having intentionality.

Formal Substance: A substance characterized in TACE as ipsi-hylomorphic, without reducing its substrate to physical matter.

Ipsi-hylomorphic: The mode of hylomorphic constitution proper to a formal substance, in which its substrate is not reduced to physical matter.

Physical Substance: A substance characterized in TACE as hetero-hylomorphic.

Hetero-hylomorphic: The mode of hylomorphic constitution in which physical Substrate and Form constitute the physical substance.

2. Realm of Forms — from Primordial Potency onward

The Realm of Forms is the formal and ontological domain in which determinations of being become intelligible.

Within TACE, it contains the structures through which being and entities can be formally determined:

Primordial Potency → Form → Entity → G-Entity Kind → G-Eupoiesis

The Tetrahedron of Entity belongs to this domain as the universal formal architecture through which an entity is determined.

Here, Entity is understood strictly as an ens in actu. Primordial Potency is therefore not itself an entity.

Primordial Potency
Universal potentiality from which determinate actualization can proceed; it is not itself an entity.
Form
The determination through which being becomes intelligibly specified.
Tetrahedron of Entity
The universal formal architecture through which the determinants of an entity are ordered.
G-Eupoiesis
The integrated resultant and center of gravity of the tetrahedral determinants.
G-Entity Kind
The determinate kind recognizable through the integrated resultant of the entity's tetrahedral determinants.
Entity
An ens in actu: a determinate being whose existence is presently actualized.

3. Physical Realm

The Physical Realm is the domain in which formal determinations become physically instantiated, acquiring physical and topological determination and participating in spatiotemporal dynamics.
