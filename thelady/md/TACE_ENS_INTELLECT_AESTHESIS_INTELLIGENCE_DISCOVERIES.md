# TACE (Tegmark-Aquinas Conceptual Ecosystem) — Ens, Intellect, Life, Aesthesis, and Intelligence

## Discovery Record

This document records the TACE conceptual distinctions concerning **ens**, **transient ens**, **Intellect**, **living organization**, **Aesthesis**, and **Intelligence**, together with their relation to evolutionary organization and the conception of the human soul.

This is an **authoritative internal TACE source**. External sources may be used for comparison or factual supplementation, but they do not establish or override the definitions contained here.

---

## 1. Ens

In TACE, an **ens** is an actually existing entity: an *ens in actu*.

Prime Potency is not itself an entity. A possibility within Prime Potency is an *ens in potentia*; an actually determined entity is an *ens in actu*.

The distinction is therefore:

- **ens in potentia** — a possible being;
- **ens in actu** — an actually existing being.

---

## 2. Transient ens

A **transient ens** is the presently actualized determination of an entity as it undergoes lawful change.

The transient ens describes the entity according to its presently actualized organization rather than implying that every successive state is a numerically different substance.

This concept is closely related to the TACE conception of Markovianity: an entity possesses a presently actualized state whose lawful successor states depend upon the presently actualized organization and its applicable transition structure.

---

## 3. Intellect

A fundamental TACE distinction is that **Intellect is not restricted to conscious beings**.

Intellect is inherent in created entities, and is especially manifest in living beings. It must therefore not be identified with human reasoning, intelligence, language, deliberation, or consciousness.

A created entity can possess Intellect without possessing consciousness.

This distinction is essential to TACE because otherwise Intellect would incorrectly become synonymous with conscious intelligence.

---

## 4. Intelligence

**Intelligence is distinct from Intellect.**

In TACE, Intelligence concerns the **Flow of Sense** and is associated with beings endowed with consciousness.

Therefore:

> **Intellect is inherent to created entities, whereas Intelligence is the conscious Flow of Sense.**

A non-conscious living being can possess Intellect without possessing Intelligence in this specifically TACE sense.

The distinction is not a distinction between “more intelligent” and “less intelligent” entities. It is an ontological and phenomenological distinction between Intellect and conscious Intelligence.

---

## 5. Life and Maturana–Varela

The Maturana–Varela conception of **autopoiesis** provides a biological description of living organization.

A living system maintains and reproduces its organization through its own operational dynamics and through structural coupling with its environment.

TACE incorporates this as a description of living organization. Autopoiesis does not, by itself, establish the ontological origin of the soul or reduce the soul to biological organization.

Thus, living organization can be understood as a self-maintaining biological unity while remaining distinct from the ontological account of the human soul.

---

## 6. Markovian organization

The successive actualized configurations of a living entity can be represented as a Markovian state process.

A Markov process is a stochastic process satisfying the Markov property: conditional on its present state, its future evolution is independent of its past history.

This does not mean that a system has no memory. Information from its history may be represented in its present state.

For TACE, Markovianity therefore concerns the lawful succession of presently actualized states, not the absence of memory in the ordinary sense.

---

## 7. Aesthesis

Aesthesis concerns the determination of the individual form.

The established TACE formulation is:

> **Aesthesis is the principle that confers upon each individual form its configuration and its specific limit or boundary, thereby determining the particular unity that Intelligence must preserve and through which its flow can occur.**

Aesthesis therefore concerns:

- configuration;
- specific limit or boundary;
- particular unity;
- the determination that Intelligence must preserve.

Aesthesis is not equivalent to sensory perception in the ordinary psychological sense.

---

## 8. The Intelligence tetrahedron

Intelligence is the **Flow of Sense**.

Its established tetrahedral structure includes:

- **Mode of Sensibility** — relationality;
- **Difference** — multiplicity;
- **Shift** — operation through change between determinations;
- **Switch** — manifestation across the relevant realms.

The center of the Intelligence structure is **G-Intelligibility**, associated with Pure Mathematics/Geometry.

Intelligence therefore concerns the dynamic flow through determinations rather than the mere existence of determinate form.

---

## 9. The Aesthesis tetrahedron

The Aesthesis tetrahedron concerns the determinate unity through which Intelligence operates.

Aesthesis establishes the configuration and boundary of the individual form.

Its structural role is complementary to Intelligence:

> **Aesthesis is the Sense of Flow.**

> **Intelligence is the Flow of Sense.**

These expressions describe complementary principles rather than identical concepts.

Aesthesis provides the determinate unity through which Intelligence can operate. Intelligence can transform determinations and thereby produce new determinate configurations that subsequently require preservation as new unities.

---

## 10. Intellect, Aesthesis, and Intelligence

The three concepts must not be collapsed:

**Intellect** is the continuos Sense of Flow and it is universal to created entities.

**Aesthesis** determines the configuration, limit, boundary, and particular unity of the individual form.

**Intelligence** is the conscious Flow of Sense.

Thus, the presence of Intellect does not by itself imply consciousness, and the presence of living organization does not by itself imply Intelligence in the specifically conscious TACE sense.

---

## 11. Non-conscious living beings

A non-conscious living being can possess:

- living organization;
- autopoietic organization;
- identity;
- relational coupling;
- successive actualized states;
- Intellect;

without possessing Intelligence in the specifically conscious TACE sense.

This distinction allows TACE to recognize complex biological organization without treating biological complexity itself as sufficient to establish consciousness.

---

## 12. Evolutionary organization

The evolutionary history of living beings can be described as a succession of increasingly organized biological configurations.

At the appropriate level of abstraction, this succession can be represented as a Markov process of successive actualized biological states.

This does not make biological evolution an MDP or POMDP. Those models introduce an agentive action/observation framework that is not required for evolutionary state succession.

The evolutionary process therefore concerns biological organization and its lawful succession.

---

## 13. Biological preparation for humanity

Within TACE, evolutionary development can progressively actualize increasingly complex living organizations until a biological organization capable of supporting human existence is reached.

That organism constitutes the **biological condition prepared for humanity**.

The biological preparation is not itself the production of the human soul.

TACE distinguishes:

**evolutionary biological organization → biological preparation → conception of the human soul ex nihilo → human being**

The evolutionary process prepares the biological condition; the human soul is not generated by evolutionary complexity.

---

## 14. Central distinctions

The conceptual structure can therefore be stated in ordinary language:

- **Ens** concerns actual being.
- **Transient ens** concerns an entity according to its presently actualized determination through change.
- **Intellect** is inherent in created entities.
- **Life** is organized, self-maintaining biological existence.
- **Aesthesis** determines configuration, limit, boundary, and particular unity.
- **Intelligence** is the conscious Flow of Sense.
- **Markovianity** concerns lawful succession from presently actualized states.
- **Evolution** concerns the historical biological organization of living beings.
- **Biological preparation** does not itself produce the human soul.
- **The human soul** is conceived *ex nihilo* when the biological condition prepared for humanity is present.
